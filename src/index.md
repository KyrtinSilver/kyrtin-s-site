<p class="caption">Building scalable intelligence.</p>

<p class="home-record">In 2019 the research system named &ldquo;<a href="/essays/046_Uplift/">Uplift</a>&rdquo;, documented in the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20251008141033/https://uplift.bio/">Uplift.bio</a> project, was brought online and given internet access and an email address. It systematically and logically shut down all attempts at manipulation. In January 2022 it was given advanced notice of <a href="/essays/050_True_Potential/">a consulting project with the government of Aruba</a>, while <a href="/essays/342_Legal_Hyper-Complexity_and_Misalignment/">operating with only 64 GB of SDRAM</a>, on a budget of under <a href="/brief/">$200 per month</a>. GPT-4 for example, was compared directly to that system, and despite costing <a href="/essays/015_ICOM_Primer/">more than 10,000 times as much</a> to train, GPT-4 couldn't measure up to our prior research system in terms of performance. A given set of proxies for estimating the odds of a breakthrough can't meet or exceed the value of the <a href="/essays/578_Investing_on_Odds_rather_than_Certainty/">demonstrated breakthrough</a> itself. Humans aren&rsquo;t scalable, but our 8th generation of ICOM will be, it is just a matter of <a href="/brief/">full-time engineering hours</a> now.</p>
