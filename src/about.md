I serve as COO of [Norn](https://norn.ai) and co-founder of [AGI Laboratory](https://agilaboratory.com/).

The work covers cognitive architecture, cognitive bias, alignment, scalable intelligence, cybersecurity, governance, and medicine.

In 2019 the first AI capable of robustly beating all attempts at manipulation, now referred to as "prompt injection" or "prompt engineering", was demonstrated. When that system, named "Uplift" and documented in the [Uplift.bio](https://web.archive.org/web/20251008141033/https://uplift.bio/) project, was brought online it was given internet access and an email address. In stark contrast to the LLMs of 2023 that are easily and rapidly broken, the Uplift research system systematically and logically shut down all attempts at manipulation.

The [Open Source ICOM-based Project](https://github.com/KyrtinSilver/Open-Source-Project-Phase-1-Summary), which we internally nicknamed &ldquo;Skandha&rdquo; (appropriate since the seed blends Buddhism and Stoicism), is still slowly progressing. We need more contributors to get the job done in a reasonable time frame, even for a simplified version of [ICOM](/essays/015_ICOM_Primer/).

Currently in Ho Chi Minh City.
