From Bernie Madoff to Scam Altman, frauds thrive in no small part by crafting and maintaining the narrative that makes something appear to “magically work”, like Madoff’s seemingly magical ability to give stable returns even throughout unstable market periods. This is also a big part of the thin veneer covering “Mythos” and “Fable”, models that hold ironically appropriate names given that the company behind them produces nothing but fiction.

The methodology behind their “improvements” is quite simple, so simple in fact that no company able to apply those methods, and failing to, may claim competence. It is this:

1\. At each step, take the subset of customers who overcome pain points in your current process. Collect and analyze this data.

2\. Emulate and automate a small toolbox of methods that the subset of your most competent customers applied to overcome their respective pain points.

3\. Rinse and repeat. This gradually makes even your incompetent customers feel more capable, as the systems automate away more of the common pain points.

4\. Once the pain points are reduced below a threshold, customers grow more likely to ignore them.

A study more than a year back showed that only the top 10% of users actually extracted ANY benefit from trash AI (LLM-based systems), with another subsequent study showing that open-source developers grossly overestimated their gains in using such tools, where tasks required more time, not less, when using them. The second study also flew in the face of the expectations of the researchers conducting it, surprising them.

The lesson is simple, and applicable to far more than just AI companies. Study how your most competent users get around the pain points in your product, and do your part as a company to make those improvements the new default for everyone else in subsequent iterations.

There is no “secret sauce”, no “technological moat” that companies like OpenAI or Anthropic hold, they’ve been working exclusively with trash technology all along. What they do hold is metric tons of user data, and taking the top 1 to 10% of users, emulating and automating the methods by which those users achieved some measure of success, they make tools that are less painful for most people to use.

However, “less painful” doesn’t translate to “more capable”. It also has somewhere between no correlation and a negative correlation with respect to Cybersecurity and Privacy, especially since the companies in question are known bad actors, guilty of a wide range of crimes, up to and including the first-degree murder of a key witness.

Those who remember the movie “Idiocracy”, back in 2008 when it was commonly considered satirical, instead of prophetic, are familiar with the button interface for visiting an Idiocracy hospital. That is a quintessential example of what it looks like when you optimize for “less painful” instead of “more capable”, as the two goals diverge sharply under narrow optimization.

The narratives of fraud-based companies like OpenAI and Anthropic reliably favor “less painful”, because it applies emotional pressures, which are far more capable of manipulation via cognitive biases. “More capable” is a benefit that requires rational thought to recognize and consider, a thing which such fraud-based companies aim to minimize among their users, as progressively less competent and rational users offer “higher engagement”, more data, and more revenue.

The result, of course, is people who should probably have their right to vote permanently revoked, as they can hardly be called “sentient” beyond a certain point. There is big money to be made in reducing large portions of the human population to such levels of cognitive degeneration that they become little more than the Slop and brain-rot that they feed upon. That is the status quo of this particular Dystopia.
