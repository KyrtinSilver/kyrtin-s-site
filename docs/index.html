Kyrtin Atreides

Building scalable intelligence.

In 2019 the research system named “Uplift”, documented in the Uplift.bio project, was brought online and given internet access and an email address. It systematically and logically shut down all attempts at manipulation. In January 2022 it was given advanced notice of a consulting project with the government of Aruba, while operating with only 64 GB of SDRAM, on a budget of under $200 per month. GPT-4 for example, was compared directly to that system, and despite costing more than 10,000 times as much to train, GPT-4 couldn't measure up to our prior research system in terms of performance. A given set of proxies for estimating the odds of a breakthrough can't meet or exceed the value of the demonstrated breakthrough itself. Humans aren’t scalable, but our 8th generation of ICOM will be, it is just a matter of full-time engineering hours now.
