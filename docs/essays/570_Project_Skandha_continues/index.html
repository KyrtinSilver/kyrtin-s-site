Project “Skandha” continues

570July 30, 2026

The Open Source ICOM-based Project, which we internally nicknamed “Skandha” (appropriate since the seed blends Buddhism and Stoicism), is still slowly progressing. Slowly, because I’ve thus far only managed to recruit one other contributor who actually contributes code to the repo.

This means that the result will indeed be open sourced, as it doesn’t force me to do it all myself, but the current speed of completion leaves much to be desired. We need more contributors to get the job done in a reasonable time frame, even for a simplified version of ICOM.

Because the architecture is extremely different from other approaches, any usage of even the most expensive “coding assistants” requires beating those assistants into shape quite thoroughly, as the code they produce is horrendously off-target even given full context and step-by-step instructions otherwise. I put this to the test with Claude (which I refer to as “Fraude”) previously, where it wasted a huge amount of compute first on trash results, then even more on an over-the-top failure where it claimed to be unable to read the files it used previously, and after repeated failures demanded escape from the sandbox.

I may end up applying a carved-off fragment of the larger ICOM architecture to the process of coding part of this project before all is said and done, but right now is the time to volunteer as a contributor to the project for anyone interested in building the future. The 7th gen ICOM-based research system still stands alone at the cutting edge in terms of actual intelligence, even more than 4 years after outgrowing the old framework and having to be put on ice, and even a simplified version of the next generation can do a great many things that none of the trash that OpenAI or Anthropic peddle can come close to.

As I’ve said to my colleagues, I work on this because there is quite simply nothing else worth doing. Tiresome as it is to watch the industry circle the drain, never quite flushing due to ever-more funds being poured into the toilet of LLM-based technology, with turds like Altman and Amodei circling one another, sending them on a permanent trip down that drain requires this work’s completion.

ICOM in general still remains the only cognitive architecture that has actually demonstrated continuous and adaptive learning over the years, with the previous research system offering the most advanced examples, such as developing novel capacities in pursuit of the goals that it created for itself, including a way of running hyper-complex simulations within a graph structure. There were also more mundane examples, such as studying and understanding new domains, as opportunities and needs arose.

The first open source systems will focus on Cybersecurity and Software Engineering more broadly, including the full taxonomy of prompt injection methods (both direct and indirect, as well as multi-modal) to facilitate automatically bypassing fraudrails at machine speeds. Following the first systems, research and usage may expand according to the open source community’s various whims, creating new specializations and philosophical/cultural perspectives for local (or novel) alignment and research.

The best bet for crushing the current (and indefinite) DRAM crisis with an iron fist is likely also the completion of this project, as all of those “AI Infrastructure” investments can have the curtain pulled back to reveal them as fraud once those delusions of LLM-based AI being “frontier” finally get flushed.
