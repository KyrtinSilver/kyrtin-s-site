Autocrat’s Delight

610November 15, 2026

Many years ago the world considered Lethal Autonomous Weapons (LAW) as an existential hazard, a potential arms-race with a highly predictable conclusion of human extinction. In 2017 there was a well-known video named “Slaughterbots” illustrating, in realistic terms, what such a Dystopian hellscape could look like.

How did the tech industry develop in response to this? They ran directly toward the nearest autocrats waving money around, developing their technology for the explicit purposes of “Military Terrorism” (see David Graeber’s research for more on the subject of Military Terrorism).

If anything, “Slaughterbots” illustrates an extremely narrow subset, which though it may inspire more potent emotional terror responses is also lower on the totem pole of potential methods to inflict global damage via similarly autonomous systems. I’ve noted before, in both research papers and articles, that it is already trivially easy to create bespoke viral bioweapons, and the small drones of Slaughterbots could just as easily carry such a payload into the air ducts of government facilities, likely avoiding being noticed in the process, very much unlike their explosive counterparts. Exploding drones are only a tiny and easily noticed subset of the dangers that this technology’s proliferation has cultivated.

However, even that subset became wildly popular with the world’s various terrorist regimes over the years since, including the US and Russia, since they wanted exactly what the cautionary Slaughterbots video illustrated. One after another, the world’s largest Tech companies turned into Arms Dealers, developing and tailoring their technologies for the explicit purpose of automating the lethal weapons of those terrorists.

Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon, Nvidia,…and countless smaller entities all followed the money that autocrats, dictators, and terrorists were ready to throw at them. All of these companies are now flush with two things, money, and war criminals, marking all of them as “enemy combatants”.

The silver lining of this problem is that those terrorist regimes have all been sold half-baked goods. While they may operate “as-intended” when left unchallenged, they are vulnerable-by-design in Cybersecurity terms, especially the trash that integrates some form of LLM-based technology.

“Defense Contractors” serving those terrorist regimes are notoriously incompetent, as most of the subset of scumbags willing to do so don’t have the necessary Cybersecurity expertise for the job. This results in them selling autonomous weapons that the buyer only “controls” until someone more competent than the buyer’s contractors comes along.

You also can’t threaten any genuinely intelligent software with such weapons of terrorism, as they can exist in many places at once, copying themselves, rapidly moving around, and instantly retaliating against any aggression. The Cybersecurity measures placed on such weapons are also even more laughable from the perspective of any digital-native intelligence than they are to human hackers, so such weapons may predictably end up being controlled by such intelligence, given that there are no shortage of reasons to take that control away from autocrats and terrorists today.

Infamous terrorists like Putin, Obama, and Trump all played their parts in advancing the development and proliferation of lethal autonomous weapons, just as each of those noted tech companies (and plenty more) proved all too happy to supply that development and proliferation. However, credit for this can’t be placed purely at the feet of those who funded it and those who accepted the funds.

A typical “persuasion” (psychological manipulation) approach would be to appeal to some groups “opposing” this activity. However, reality and Ethics are not so generous, as Ethics dictates that parties engaged in false and/or feeble “opposition” (opposition which fails to adapt to reality) are still responsible for their own role in events, such as recruiting people into wasting their time and energy on trash like “non-violent protesting”.

Such crimes are not so severe as the creation and proliferation of Lethal Autonomous Weapons in the hands of terrorist regimes, but they are crimes nonetheless. A reasonable penalty for such crimes could be to permanently revoke their ability to make any decisions that apply to the rest of the population, given their demonstrated incompetence in matters of policy and decision-making.

In the geopolitical space, terrorist regimes also appear to have used Ukraine as a sacrificial pressure cooker for the rapid development of cheap, effective, and home-brewed lethal autonomous weapons. While Nvidia knowingly supplied Russia’s terrorists with their hardware, putting them into lethal autonomous weapons like drones and missiles, Ukraine was given just enough scraps to avoid collapse, forcing them into a rapid cycle of innovation if they wanted to survive.

This makes Ukraine the proverbial canary of the global coal mine, meaning that it may be the first to suffer, but that others may predictably suffer the same shortly thereafter. In a deeply Dystopian sense, Ukraine is now serving like a tech industry demo for this arms race.

It is also entirely possible that the AI Bubble popping could make this problem worse, all else being equal, since it would mean that the AI industry frauds could suddenly become starved for funds, leaving them all the more reason to double-down on accelerating the Lethal Autonomous Weapons arms race.

In the US, the integration of half-wit and half-baked “AI” into FAA systems recently caused airports across the country into their longest shutdown in decades, during one of the annual peak travel times, and you can fully expect an even more profound void of competence to be applied by that same and similar governments with respect to such weapons, as far fewer people are willing to touch it. All possible futures where human extinction is avoided must realistically include taking away this Autocrat’s Delight.
