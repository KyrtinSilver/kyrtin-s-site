In the Absence of Plot Armor

514May 1, 2026

Just a quick note for all of those aspiring assassins of the tech bros and terrorist regimes of major countries, go read some f^&*ing Sun Tzu and don’t do the dumbest thing that you possibly can. It isn’t even that long of a text, you can read it in a single day. If you can’t manage that, you shouldn’t even be responsible for dressing yourself in the morning.

Luigi Mangioni, who was in severe chronic pain, and even as the “Eiffel 65 of executive assassins” (one-hit wonder), he vastly outperformed the half-wits who attacked Scam Altman’s house, not the guy himself, his f&*^ing house, or the most recent White House Correspondents Dinner “shooter” (scoring a solid “0”), who couldn’t be bothered to establish himself in the Hilton before the security perimeter was put up. A Chatbot without the faintest shred of actual intelligence could have done better.

For example, consider the most basic math of some situation with a bunch of violent criminals wearing bulletproof vests. Lets say that they wear bulletproof vests 8 hours per day, every workday. Now, what would Sun Tzu do? Would he attack those violent criminals, using a gun, during the window of time when they are wearing bulletproof vests?

No, he wouldn’t. That is something a complete idiot would do. Real life doesn’t give you “plot armor”, so you don’t recover from rolling a critical failure on your Stupid Check. You’re not The Punisher, the Boondock Saints, or V for Vendetta. Writing your beliefs into a “manifesto” doesn’t give you the plot armor of your own narrative. Instead of plot armor you either have competence, or you don’t.

Yes, “this is why we can’t have nice things”. When a criminal enterprise, public or private, controls the opposition in true Soviet style, bringing a little tear to Lenin’s eyes, that opposition tends to critically fail the Stupid Check like clockwork.

Even as the ease of such revolutionary warfare has steadily increased in the past decade, owing to the increasing hypercomplexity of society and the laughably primitive systems of surveillance capitalism’s “automation”, the competence of such aspiring revolutionaries has tumbled toward rock bottom just as fast, if not faster.

The dark comic absurdity of it all only goes deeper with each passing year. A criminal regime puts 18th century revolutionaries on a pedestal, very literally treating their words as law, while also making 21st century revolutionaries highly illegal. Both 18th century and 21st century revolutionaries are, by definition, “treasonous”, but only one is treated as such.

Survivorship Bias plays a role in how such individuals are later perceived, through lenses of success or failure. Population dynamics often takes it from there.

As I’ve written in previous years, one of the most noble ways that someone can dedicate their life (hard), or death (easy), is to prevent others from needing to face the hardships that they themselves once faced. I referred to this concept as “Noble Revenge”, to protect others from such harm, at whatever scale and level of permanence is feasible for a given individual.

Only those who do far better, in life or death, may claim any “noble” purpose.
