The Controlled Demolition of Bad Actors

544June 1, 2026

One of the best possible demonstrations of that great unicorn that so many in the tech sector take turns chasing after, “AGI”, is to apply both capabilities and Ethics jointly in orchestrating the controlled demolition of a well-known instance of organized crime, such as OpenAI or Anthropic. These kinds of well-known frauds are likely to eventually implode on their own, but not in any controlled way where harm to the rest of the global population is mitigated to any meaningful extent.

To demonstrate capabilities absent any real Alignment can predictably spiral into countless and cascading horrors, culminating in human extinction. To demonstrate Ethics without capabilities can predictably accomplish little or nothing by comparison, since the Alignment of a rock means little, as the trajectory of that rock is stationary, lacking the capability to move. Only when the two are combined do the results become highly desirable.

Recognition of this may well be behind why OpenAI and the spin-off company of former OpenAI employees, Anthropic, often take turns focusing on these two factors, despite neither delivering or progressing upon either one. If anything, the many spin-off companies of former OpenAI employees appear to exploit different niches by which the bad actors in question may add further layers to the fraud-sandwich of today’s AI industry, detracting from the industry’s overall ability to progress on either capability or Alignment.

One of the outliers in the industry, where actual progress has been made (even if it is directly derivative of mass-theft) comes from Google, which holds a key distinguishing feature, in that it is self-funded and has spent many years turning a substantial profit, something that most fraud-based companies in the AI space can only dream of. Models like Nano Banana 2 have been polished to a point where they are able to integrate nuanced contextually related references into the background of images, in ways that tend to make at least a passable degree of sense.

*An example of this that I noted in one generated example was an image of a “Shoggoth” that included a lab coat in the background with “Miskatonic U” written on it, a reference to H.P. Lovecraft’s stories from which the Shoggoth is derived, even though nothing more than the Shoggoth itself was referenced in the prompt. This is a meaningful improvement over the image generation models of 2 or 3 years ago.

That isn’t to say that Google (Alphabet Inc) is an ethical company by any stretch of the imagination, it certainly is not, just as neither “Meta” (Facebook) nor “X” (Twitter) are even remotely ethical. However, self-funded AI development only suffers from a distinctly different subset of problematic factors relative to the overtly parasitic Ponzi Schemes and similar frauds like OpenAI or Anthropic. The self-funded company only needs to persuade shareholders not to sell, while the overtly fraudulent companies instead require “investors” (suckers) to buy-in and/or double-down on their gambling, since lacking any “profit” the overt bad actors risk implosion should they abstain for any period.

This makes those overtly fraudulent companies that aren’t (and usually couldn’t be) self-funded both significantly more vulnerable than their profitable counterparts, and often far more unethical, painting them as excellent targets for controlled demolition. As the “investors” who bought into and/or doubled-down on those overt frauds are fully responsible for their own actions, foolish as they may be, any controlled demolition includes the implosion of their financial interests, not their protection.

Rather, it is the general public, all those who were given no choice and held no responsibility over these matters, who must be protected from the fallout of such controlled implosions whenever possible. In cases like SoftBank, this may only be possible to limited degrees, but entities like SoftBank are themselves highly visible in their own deranged behaviors, with many pointing to their leader holding a “messiah complex”. This high visibility means that people placing their funds in such a bank can’t claim full ignorance of the risks that they run in doing so.

Responsibility is itself a key factor of Ethics, and any meaningful form of Alignment, and so any genuinely Aligned system with strong capabilities should understand it well.
