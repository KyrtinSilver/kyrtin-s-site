A Healthy AImmune-system

573August 8, 2026

My PSA this quarter for any remaining humans out there: Keeping your body and mind healthy, with a healthy separation from AI Slop, is one of the better defenses against AI Psychosis.

Most of it is common sense that people have developed the habit of neglecting, like exercise, healthy eating, and moderation in the media that they consume, but all of it adds up. The various detriments to mental and physical health, like smoking, alcohol, drugs, or digital bingeing such as YouTube (often at the detriment of sleep) are all comorbidities that can greatly increase a wide range of other health risks.

The book “Why We Sleep” by a leading sleep scientist, Matthew Walker, has been required reading for my team across many years. Besides the habits required for healthy sleep, a daily exercise routine, particularly first thing in the morning, offers potent health benefits.

According to a variety of studies, healthy habits usually take ~3 weeks to pass through each of 3 stages of growing established, beyond which they become far easier to maintain. In practice, this means it is also much easier to integrate no more than 1 new healthy habit per 3-4 weeks, and linking this to a monthly schedule further increases the ease of maintenance.

Other studies have demonstrated that routines with fixed anchors are far easier to maintain, such as “Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday”, as opposed to “every other day”, or “every other Monday”.

It is also worth noting how false dichotomies have become entrenched in places like the US, where bad actors populate (and frequently dominate) both sides of the coin, such as the “Alternative” vs “Clinical” health industries in the US. This isn’t the case across most of the rest of the world, but the US has a population that is higher on wealth and lower on competence, making it fertile ground for such schemes.

In most places, healthy habits are simple things that nobody sells to you. You don’t go to Whole Foods (aka “Whole Paycheck”) to get special foods, you just eat ordinary healthy things with regularity.

I also strongly recommend no more than 5 minutes per week of exposure to AI Slop content, and preferably as close to zero as possible. For those who have to deal with “Generative AI” for work, setting a strict physical work environment where you only engage with such systems and content in that specific environment can help significantly.

When the AI Bubble does pop, there are likely to be a lot of former humans out there who never really recover. There are plenty of things worse than death, making AI Psychosis measurably worse than Covid, and no less contagious among vulnerable populations.

Don’t make yourself a vulnerable population through neglecting your own mental and physical health. Choose not to eat (and by eating, become) the Slop. You are the sum of the choices that you make, including all physical and digital things that you consume, so choose the healthy habits by which you may continue to improve.
