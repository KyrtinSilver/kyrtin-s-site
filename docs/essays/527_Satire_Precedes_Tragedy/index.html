Satire Precedes Tragedy

527May 15, 2026

“I’ll see to it that he learns that arresting thieves is against the law.” – Terry Pratchett, “Guards! Guards!” (1989)

While Terry undoubtedly wrote this phrase in satire, decades later, not only did it become reality, numerous variations upon the same did as well.

During my years in Seattle, a documentary called “Seattle is Dying” (as well as a sequel to it) came out, detailing one such problem. Seattle is one example of a city with a self-created and perpetuated drug problem, where illicit drugs and related crime run rampant, and have for many years now. I saw this first-hand, especially when I lived in downtown Seattle.

In that case, the Seattle City Council, and their various criminal accomplices, made it impossible for drug-related laws to be enforced, and punished the only police officer to so much as issue $20 citations for illegal drug use in public. This in turn facilitated a long sequence of escalating absurdities to unfold over the years, a situation mirrored in other similar cities, such as San Francisco.

Elsewhere and more recently in the US, such as Minneapolis, the enforcement of laws related to murder and other forms of violence were abandoned, as masked and heavily armed thugs roamed the streets of major urban areas, under orders from a convicted felon whose face is as orange as some prison jumpsuits, which he should be wearing today.

In 2022, Sam Bankman-Fried was sent to prison, but his criminal accomplices (Anthropic’s co-founders) who laundered $500m for him were mysteriously ignored, despite everyone knowing about it before the end of that year. Even when they later added verified piracy and book-burning to that rap sheet, still these criminals have avoided prison.

Whether related to drugs, violence, or theft, all such forms of criminality have come to be guarded by (and often milked via rent-seeking by) criminal accomplices in positions of governmental “authority”.

Perhaps nowhere is this more clear than in matters of war, and the flagrant violation of both national and international laws. This has not only occurred, but occurred so frequently as to be normalized in some places, where rulers are no longer expected to follow any rules.

A law requires that the US president can’t unilaterally drag the nation into war, and that should one do so they must answer for it 60 days later. This was ignored.

By international law, the International Criminal Courts (ICC) issued a warrant for the arrest of an infamous war criminal nicknamed “Bibi” in 2024. This too was ignored, even with his itinerary (and accomplices) known.

Both were matters of war crimes, and just like cases related to drugs, theft, and lesser scales of violence, both were ignored. If you’re waiting for humanity to hit “rock bottom”, this looks to be pretty close to it, at least for the regions in question. Almost anything can get a little bit worse, but this is scraping the bottom.

As I noted previously, a much larger number of governments do indeed directly protect criminals from the repercussions of their own criminality, in violation of their own stated intentions. For them, reprisals against thieves are already plainly illegal, leaving the offending governing bodies with no credibility to speak of.

Absent credibility, no “legal authority” can exist in actuality. Violent thugs may claim some authority while threatening force, while others may throw slanderous and equally baseless accusations, but none may compensate for that absence of credibility.
