Blindness to Conditional Risk

572August 3, 2026

Clickbait masquerading as scientific material is everywhere, now AI-optimized for mass-slopification. A recent example of this was brought to my attention, where an article claimed that sunscreen caused higher rates of skin cancer.

I was immediately reminded of the XKCD comic titled “Conditional Risk”, which demonstrates the same actual effect (a cognitive bias known as the “Peltzman Effect”), where the awareness of reduced risk-per-unit results in individuals increasing their exposure to those risks.

Long-term studies like those looking for cancer rates don’t, and can’t, control for sun exposure over the prolonged periods required, ignoring the Peltzman Effect. This can quickly lead to some idiot claiming that a safety measure causes the problem, failing to account for all of the individuals who greatly increased their risk exposure due to the perception of increased safety.

This problem of Conditional Risk and the Peltzman Effect also applies very acutely (albeit often silently) to the AI domain, due to the massive emphasis on “Security Theater”, also known as “guardrails” (fraudrails).

When the bad actors pushing Security Theater systematically increase the perception of safety and security (while simultaneously degrading actual safety and security) the Conditional Risks escalate at an exponential rate, rapidly growing in both number and severity.

Even assuming the best of intentions on the part of certain AI companies (an entrenched delusion not worth broaching), the problem has escalated to the point of these companies openly competing on the number of felonies that they’ve committed, rather than their previous bread and butter crimes of theft and money laundering.

This problem of dynamics is further accelerated when multiple bad actors with differing goals are freely able to exert such influence upon individuals, businesses, and governments. It then becomes a “Tragedy of the Commons”, where humans are the “shared resources” being greedily sliced up and devoured at the expense of any future worth having.

Much like the 2008 financial crisis, massive Real Estate risks are being created via so-called “AI Infrastructure”, not with sub-prime loans to poor families, but with sub-prime loans to massive corporations that have never turned a profit, propped up only by perpetual cash infusions from “investors”, the Bernie Madoff way.

The “Cognitive Offloading” to unintelligent and/or malevolent systems is nothing particularly new, as people have believed marketing and news alike in plenty of cases where they shouldn’t for more than a century, but those who eat from the feed bag strapped to their head rather than applying their own cognition will believe in complete nonsense, progressing in their “brainrot”, right up until the lightning strikes them.
