Geopolitical Stability vs Volatility in Tech

579September 1, 2026

The EU is often criticized for “Regulation” in the Tech/AI space, but both those in favor of it and against it completely miss the real pros and cons of US vs EU vs EE (Everywhere Else). That very same glacial slowness that the EU is often criticized for is a massive benefit compared to the US’s absolutely maximized volatility. Lets walk through the basics:

  • In the EU a simple “AI Act” takes years to move from concept to reality, and when it finally gets applied it is so watered down and full of loopholes as to be meaningless, making it largely fake regulation in actuality. However, what it does offer is extreme stability.

  • In the US, a dictator can randomly issue an “Executive Order” on any given day, even if it is purely based on a slight to his pride on social media, going so far as to cause massive damage to global markets, as seen in the openly criminal “Tariff” bullshit.

  • In other countries, they might draw some inspiration from these two parties, but results tend to fall in the middle ground, lacking the multi-cultural and glacial stability of the EU, or the combination of an Authoritarian Regime entrenched by over 7 decades of global Military Terrorism.

The main excuse for remaining (or founding) in the US that I’ve heard thus far relies on the ability to bribe the Authoritarian regime, whose local opposition is laughable, having decayed over decades. Of course, bribes have a habit of increasing, and they leave whoever makes them squarely under the thumb of a dictator, subject to that regime’s terrorism.

In cases where there is a high population of “former CIA” (not all of whom are truly off of the payroll), any attempts at secrecy will tend to backfire, causing those bribes to rapidly skyrocket and technology to be hijacked by such a dictator, sooner or later.

Some criminal organizations have played into this, offering that Authoritarian regime stock and exclusive access to their “most powerful” (but still largely fraudulent) technology, as seen in the cases of OpenAI and Anthropic. It is the laughability of the technology that those criminal organizations peddle which makes them generally not worth the trouble of wrecking.

Frankly, the US is among the worst choices of location globally, as even if one wanted to pursue the bribery of a regime, the bribes would tend to be higher in the US, and the panopticon more complete. Maximized risks from instability and the hyper-concentration of authoritarian “power” make for one spectacularly nasty soup, making virtually all other location-based risks lesser in comparison.

The next time that someone jokes about the EU’s regulation of AI, laugh hard at that person specifically, and make it clear that they are the joke, not what they said. Such spectacular stupidity deserves no better, and such people shouldn’t be trusted to undertake any critical thinking, now or in the future.
