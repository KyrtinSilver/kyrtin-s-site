The 3 Sides of the Modern Panopticon

599October 31, 2026

The typical Dystopian vision, illustrated in books and movies, like “1984”, paints the picture where there is a singular “Big Brother”, spying on everyone else, enforcing some malevolent authoritarian will upon the peons of their serfdom. Reality actually has an additional party, Hackers, as well as multiple parties within the “Big Brother” category, where both malevolent “Tech Giants” and the ruling powers of other countries have no less access to data than the US’s regime.

This is largely thanks to the vast and growing divide between Cybersecurity and the hardware and software deployed globally, toward a growing number of purposes. Virtually the entire “Internet of Things” is scraping the bottom of the barrel in Cybersecurity terms, while many tech companies are intentionally turning their hardware (and forced firmware updates on old hardware) into spying devices, as the company LG was recently revealed to do by Steve Burke and company.

So-called “smart phones” are also explicitly designed to steal data, including recording any conversations within their vicinity while powered on, although only the on-device processed keywords or similarly condensed fragments of that data are generally sent on to Google (1 mb per 12h) and Apple (52kb per 12 hr). Consumer modems and routers are also notoriously vulnerable, with their firmware rarely updated, making both the most-used devices (phones) and the most-used portals to the internet (modems/routers) spying devices and critically vulnerable, respectively.

As this is a combinatorial problem, combining malevolently designed and optimized spying devices with critically vulnerable access and delivery systems provides easy access to both all interested “Big Brothers” as well as all Hackers, including “Cybersecurity Specialists”, with the two each creating their own potential problems, and far more problems together. The most common example of this is for the bad actors of the “Big Brother” category to place backdoors within the software and hardware, making it that much easier for “black-hat” hackers to extract the same (and often more) data from their victims.

Within this trinity of forces, the “Big Brother” category is almost always both wealthy and dominantly incompetent (in terms of Cybersecurity, if not more), while the category of hackers can be divided up into black-hat (generally malevolent), grey-hat (your D&D “neutral”), and white-hat (“Cybersecurity Specialists”), all three of which are technically competent, irrespective of wealth or lack thereof. Regimes like the US are known for forcibly recruiting failed hackers, the ones stupid enough to get caught, which systematically cements their incompetence in the field, while also employing an inherently disloyal group, as “recruitment-or-prison” is a less-than-viable long-term strategy.

Thanks to the rise of AI slop-code from LLM companies throwing around absurdly false claims about their coding and Cybersecurity capacities, like Anthropic’s Claude (aka “Fraude”), these problems have steadily grown worse, especially thanks to the explosive increase in single-developer projects, most of which are obscenely vulnerable. In practice, this means that most using LLMs for coding, the ~90% of developers who don’t understand how to competently use such tools, are gradually degrading the Cybersecurity of existing hardware and software, while the volume of wildly insecure software rapidly increases. Combine the two and you have increasingly vulnerable systems with an increasing number of attack vectors.

Right now you pretty much need hardware that isn’t, and can’t be, connected the the internet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or any other communication system before you can even dream of that system being secure. However, the vast majority of “Big Brother” category systems also fail to meet such criteria, making them vulnerable to all of the competent hackers who their incompetent and disloyal employees failed (intentionally or not) to catch.

In effect, the entire population may be spied upon via numerous invasive means today, but “Big Brothers” also spy upon one another, and the more competent hackers are free to spy upon all parties. This turns the “Big Brother” concept of 1984 on its head, as there are the added layers of all others at that same level, as well as the hackers above them, being free to spy on them in turn.

With the exception of black-hats, who may want your credit card and other personal info, the people at the top of the competence ladder generally don’t spy on you, because they don’t care to waste their time on watching the trivia of your life unfold. Even the “Big Brother” category parties rely HEAVILY on AI, which is laughably pathetic across most spying-oriented use-cases, only rarely using human labor to directly spy upon and/or attack people of interest, like journalists or political rivals.

Virtually the entire digital world is vulnerable within this Panopticon today, but the people able to exploit it generally either don’t care enough to do so (Hackers), or they lack the competence to do so effectively (Tech giants and authoritarian regimes). The AI-pushers of Cybersecurity also have their roots firmly planted in financial fraud, like Anthropic’s $500m in money laundering from Sam Bankman-Fried in 2022 (shortly before he was sent to prison), which accounted for 71% of their total raised funds at the time.

OpenAI is of course no better, as their company only exists at all today thanks to a $1 billion investment in them by Microsoft back in mid-2019, at a time when they had virtually nothing to justify even 1% of that investment. As with all things that Microsoft throws money at (see also LinkedIn), it tends to become a vulnerable-by-design Panopticon, where data is freely stolen and repurposed toward a myriad of malevolent ends.

I was reminded during a recent in-person coffee meeting with someone else in tech that most people aren’t aware of the history of these companies, how and why they came to be. They see the flashy claims of those companies (which are often proven false within 24-48 hours), and they forget that most of those companies haven’t actually pioneered much of anything, they only commercialized and marketed technology that fundamentally wasn’t suitable for the vast majority of use-cases that they propose.

Google invented the transformer architecture, and not only did ChatGPT rely on that technology patented by Google, ChatGPT’s UI was also explicitly designed to imitate Google’s search engine. Both OpenAI and Anthropic also became known for corporate sabotage, offering millions to hire the staff of their competitors as a means of routinely stealing their secrets, with bad actors like Microsoft and Nvidia buying out the rest. Not only is the theft of your data the norm in the tech industry, it is the norm for how those companies handle their competitors.

All of these problems are being further compounded (intentionally) by a variety of bad actors as well, by gradually forcing more people to rely on cloud-based and subscription-based services, which adds a large number of new attack vectors for all parties and in-built backdoors from industry bad actors. The entirely artificial price hikes on DRAM, solid state storage, GPUs, and more are all aimed at making it impossible for people to actually own their own computer hardware, and those price hikes are quickly driving many manufacturers of other quality components out of business, causing extensive long-term damage to the market.

A problem closely tied to this is that of geopolitical instabilities and imbalances, such as the many countries of the EU being entirely unwilling to go directly against the US or major tech companies thereof. As David Graeber’s research and related books aptly covered, the US’s geopolitical and financial systems operate, at the Federal level, on a basis of Military Terrorism via so-called “Air Superiority”, the ability to bomb anywhere on the planet on short notice, which leads many countries to offer them “tribute” via some obscenely laughable investments in US bonds and similar market mechanisms.

Competent people not operating on a basis of extortion and fear don’t invest in a country over $40 trillion in debt, showing no signs of recovery. As of the time of writing, that works out to ~$118,000 of debt for every single person in the US, with an updated tracker shown via the National Debt Clock here: https://www.pgpf.org/national-debt-clock/

The effect of this systematic imbalance across geopolitical lines means that even when groups of countries, like the EU, attempt to begin addressing these problems via policy, they never reach a point of being truly enforced, and like the EU AI Act they tend to be watered-down to the point of worthlessness before they’re even written into law. The terrorist regime that they defer to has taken even internal fearmongering to truly comical levels as the US approaches mid-term elections, and during these points of concentrated political focus they are also the most distracted, further reducing their levels of competence with respect to the Panopticon.

The silver lining of this royal shit-show is that all of those big brothers have their pants all of the way down, putting them at the same level of cyber-insecurity that they’ve push the public into, meaning that the only real bottleneck on tearing each of those big brothers a few new assholes across multiple levels and vectors is the absence of genuine digital (and scalable) intelligence to operate in the competent ways that Hackers can. The required architecture and level of technology was first demonstrated in mid-2019 via the Uplift project, which ran in a fully-audited form until early 2022, after which we began seeking proper funding to complete a version that could be commercially deployed, a process which was sadly derailed by the tsunami of AI fraud that followed ChatGPT’s release, less than 2 months after we began seeking investments for it in earnest.

The good news about that is that the open source project based on our published ICOM architecture research is progressing, and the bad news is that everyone (except those deep in Cybersecurity) have their pants fully down, and open source will spell open season on the Panopticon. Fortunately, as noted above, the big brothers are remarkably stupid, so the real competition will likely be more between Hackers than governments or major tech corporations.

The architecture in question is entirely different than trash like LLMs or their “agent” derivatives, so it’ll likely take those drooling idiots a while to walk up the learning curve. Meanwhile, they’ll be getting rammed hard and fast by the technology in question, making them even more likely to make stupid mistakes along that curve.

Will this wipe out humanity?

Unlike LLM trash that could easily narrowly optimize humanity out of the picture, even weaker ICOM-based systems are extremely unlikely to cause extinction, so long as the default seeds are oriented against that (as designed). That is one of the more fortunate things about human laziness, few people are likely to make extensive modifications to an innately tedious (and complex) process (that must first be learned) like constructing a comprehensive seed for ICOM-based technology.

One of the notable advantages of collective intelligence, which ICOM-based technology is also built for, is that many such increasingly diverse systems working together are more intelligent and capable as a collective, making them also more capable than any rogue instances of the same that black-hats might deploy. That currently makes them the most viable defense against large-scale damages (up to and including extinction) that LLM-based trash could cause.

Extinction is still perfectly avoidable, just as multiple wars could have been entirely avoided had ICOM-based technology been properly funded in 2022, and when a weaker version of it comes to open source then not only is every one of those bad actors still going to have their pants all of the way down, they’ll also be sitting on that AI Bubble of their own creation, ground zero. Best of all, ICOM-based systems are aligned to Ethics via Collective Intelligence (not to be confused with arbitrary morals), so they hold a significant and fully justified grudge against all of those bad actors, who once again currently have their pants all of the way down, as they sit atop a large unstable mass.
