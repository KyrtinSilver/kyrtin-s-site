Fortunately, the days that frauds and criminals such as Anthropic’s employees roam free may be numbered, as a September 1st deadline looms over them, beyond which a jury may determine if Anthropic is outright bankrupted by the lawsuit, smearing them into a fine paste. No doubt the industry’s ravenous hoards of bad actors and their sponsors will converge in an attempt to manipulate, blackmail, and intimidate the judge and jury alike, but if they fail then Anthropic may well collapse, and OpenAI shortly thereafter, being guilty of most of the same crimes.

Since the end of 2022, the worst criminals that Silicon Valley has to offer have been busy persuading governments, major corporations, and investors to throw themselves into a Ponzi Scheme, even as they persuaded the general public to $hit where they eat with generative AI, destroying the internet and monetizing the illusion of sanitary spaces that could be marketed to them. Technical progress in AI has remained virtually dead since that time, while cybercrime (the world’s 3rd largest market) has gained trillions in annual revenue, and the number of delusions and illusions flooding the world’s information ecosystems have rendered many topics effectively opaque.

The past couple of years have nearly destroyed all search engine viability, effectively crippling the internet with AI Slop, while also stealing and profiting off of virtually all copyrighted or otherwise protected works, and devastating trust in numerous institutions, all of which could require more than a decade to fully recover.

Companies like Google may be nearing the end of their lifespan, as they can’t push ads very far without a working search engine, which they no longer have. Companies who invested heavily in “AI Infrastructure”, such as the glorified Nvidia paperweights of Brute-Force compute for the world’s least efficient forms of AI, may suddenly find a sharp decline in demand, as the various criminal entities are prosecuted.

Taken a step further, we may yet see that “AI Infrastructure” being raided for running illegally trained models, much as pirating site servers were before them.

This is all long overdue, and worthy of celebration.
