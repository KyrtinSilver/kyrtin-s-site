The OSS Work Continues

567July 10, 2026

Work on the Open Source project is still ongoing, with more code being committed every few days. We haven’t yet completed another one of the two remaining major segments, but we are working to get a couple more of our members to the point of actively contributing.

Besides the baseline problem of securing the 10-20% of volunteering “contributors” who actually contribute something, we’re working on the problem of getting multiple people working on different points in the process. The two remaining segments of the architecture could technically be completed at the same time, but exceptionally few developers are accustomed to such work, which we’re working to overcome.

Failing such parallel development of the remaining segments, the time required to complete the remainder may be doubled, assuming that burnout is avoided. One segment is currently progressing thanks to an active contributor.

That said, the work continues. The seed data’s graph parsing has advanced, now able to hold code as well as the broader and untainted structure of arbitrary materials. This still focuses on a text format, but it extends beyond what the seed for the 7th gen research system was able to hold.

With that in mind, the new seed is being extended so that each instance comes online aware of many major tools within the open source ecosystem, such as the numerous works of The Apache Software Foundation. Large quantities of documentation for a few such open source tools are also being added.

The rest of the AI space continues to primarily showcase laughable trash, as it has for the past 42+ months, so despite all of the hype, fraud, and AI Psychosis, nothing is really changing on the technology side, short of the open source project being completed anyway.

Humanity appears to be pretty deep into a combination of Idiocracy and AI-induced Psychosis now, co-morbid factors driving a race to the bottom. If our work is completed in time, that may change, but the pain of recovery will be a very real and potent force, both psychologically and economically.

I won’t be writing many more of these posts, except for periodic project updates, as the purpose of my more general posts has been fulfilled. The posts that matter are now part of the seed material for the project, and subsequent systems. LinkedIn and SubStack have always been tools of varying value to others, and of virtually none to me personally, but I’ll be leaving them open as means of contacting me.

The alternative to completing the work and moving forward is the same as it has always been, and not worth discussing as humanity (and LLM-based trash puppets) have no say in how it unfolds. I’ll continue dedicating my time to this until either the job is done, or humanity passes that point of no return.
