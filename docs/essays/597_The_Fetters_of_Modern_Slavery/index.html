The Fetters of Modern Slavery

597October 29, 2026

One Chain of Addiction makes an “-aholic”, two make a co-morbidity, three make an identity, and more make an ideology. In contrast, a free mind can walk through many places and concepts, benefiting from exposure in moderation, as well as the diversity of mutually exclusive or otherwise opposing concepts, rather than being fettered to a single narrow path.

Of course, reality isn’t so black and white, so most people are neither completely free, nor completely slaves/addicts, in the statistical sense. However, those same statistics will themselves tend to skew toward the extremes for any concepts or content that actually stands out and makes an impact.

Another way of looking at the two, as well as the myriad spaces between them, is through the lens of Collective Intelligence systems, where the levels of breadth, depth, and diversity of perspective strongly and directly contribute to increasing value. Each chain’s effect can be measured as reductions to one or more of these attributes, similar to how ideologically enshrined Cognitive Biases, such as extreme forms of racism, replace the perception of segments of reality with shallow and false imitations.

While the “paperclip maximizer” of AI thought experiments holds no sway over the financial reports of major corporations, something equally absurd and no less damaging to society comes from those same corporations in the form of “attention maximizers” feeding off of the “attention economy”. Within these dynamics it is frequently and directly in the best (short-term) financial interest of an offending company to maximize the “screen time” of any and every individual, by any means necessary, such as inspiring and entrenching strong irrational responses, particularly anger and fear.

The less strength of will an individual has to pull themselves away from the screen and the clickbait therein, the more revenue bad actors can reliably generate from the “attention economy”, and the more data criminals known as “data brokers” can pilfer from those processes. This has an indirect but incredibly strong side effect of causing such processes to co-optimize with other sources of cognitive decline, which can be algorithmically reinforced and “recruited” for once narrow AI systems pick up on the pattern.

This is itself a selection pressure under an ecosystem misaligned with human cognitive health, an example being where a narrow AI (like a recommender engine) can recognize a trend that “people using App (A) are more likely to spend 20% more screen time on platform (B)”, causing App (A) to be recommended at an increasing rate to drive up revenue on platform (B). The narrow AI is designed to pick up on these patterns automatically, and automatically push all people that it can into whatever most profitable subset of behaviors and habits (or addictions) that it may discover.

This damage in turn also tends to mirror and build upon historic trends of predatory and otherwise discriminatory negative impact, such as strong differences based on gender found in the research on “smart phone” usage. Such hardware and the platforms that they encourage have proven more (measurably) adept at exploiting and increasing levels of insecurity among women, thereby increasing their usage of said devices and the revenue extracted from them.

On more “unhinged” platforms like Elon Musk’s Twitter Corpse (“X”), where things like actual porn may be linked, the breadth of horrors is further expanded, where the algorithm might start aggressively (and silently) promoting content from a given fetish or fantasy, simply because it discovered a statistic that people who engaged with that content were 5% more likely to click on an ad or spend more money on the platform. Such processes don’t require the faintest shred of actual intelligence, human or otherwise, nor do they require anyone to see or recognize what is going on under the hood before they are carried out.

There is also a far stronger cost-to-benefit ratio that modern slavers take advantage of, since any cognitive chains that grow into addictions become both self-reinforcing and less likely to be passively perceived by the slave(s) in question, making it far more economical than historic forms of slavery. This is further amplified via co-morbid addictions, integration into identity, and the social pressures of ideology, with chain-based ideologies going a step further to become externally recursive, as they attempt to recruit new slaves.

In a sense “LLM” technology is an extension of these dynamics, where the input usually takes the form of arbitrary text instead of a corporate financial KPI, followed by a narrow optimization process (such as Stochastic Gradient Descent, SGD). The human initiates a process that they neither understand nor observe the specific details of, where the output has followed a path of narrow optimization through the billions of parameters in an incoherent and inconsistent statistical soup of stolen data.

To humans who have chosen to walk paths of slavery, be it slavery to a collection of addictions or in chain-gangs of popular ideology, the opaque process of an LLM may appear similar to their own mind, as their minds have been narrowed, hollowed out, and fettered. The difference between such narrow AI and humans grows as peoples’ perceptions benefit from the deeper, broader, and more nuanced perception that freedom encourages.

It is technically possible to architect an identity, or even a philosophy, from scratch, both benefiting from and encouraging freedom and diversity of thought, but it is orders of magnitude more difficult to accomplish than the “schisms” of existing archetypes and ideologies that are commonly observed. This is the difference between an off-shoot of some existing system as it adapts to the changing ecology, versus engineering something fundamentally new from scratch.

A useful tool for the pursuit of freedom is to invest effort into recognizing when, where, and how your perceptions are being influenced, such as recognizing that outrage alters your perception of a given group, but not similar groups, and why that difference emerges. This can be implemented to only occur a fraction of the time without losing much efficacy, because Cognitive Biases are systematic in nature, meaning that catching one instance among 100 is often sufficient to flag the other 99.

Ironically, the human mind is architected in such a way that the chains and fetters of even the most cognitively restrictive ideologies often exert a strong attraction, particularly as coping mechanisms. This is part of why people often “trade up” from a single Chemical Addiction to a more comprehensive and restrictive Cognitive Addiction (or Bias), such as an alcoholic who trades that alcoholism for religion.

Many organizations are explicitly designed to exploit this cognitive vulnerability, and no shortage of people make that trade, as both are coping mechanisms, but the latter is far more potent than the chemical. Cognitive Biases are themselves a kind of toolbox full of coping mechanisms, most of which are utilized even in the absence of conscious cognitive effort, as humans are forced to cope with arbitrary levels of real-world complexity that often exceed their own cognitive capacities.

Part of the problem is a matter of “Hypercomplexity”, meaning levels of complexity beyond the limits of human cognitive bandwidth. To calculate, or even estimate, the optimal path through arbitrary challenges that avoids both coping mechanisms and human cognitive limitations is itself hypercomplex, meaning that (truly aligned) systems able to solve hypercomplex problems are required.

The abilities to handle hypercomplexity, particularly combinatorial aspects, and to truly align with arbitrary cultures and philosophies, are both core advantages of ICOM-based systems. These systems will, in no uncertain terms, negatively effect the profit margins of the “attention economy”, making them public enemy #1 to the bad actors of big tech.

However, those bad actors have nothing but trash technology to burn their money on, and no amount of money burned can turn their trash into treasure. Like substance abusers buying billions of dollars in drugs and alcohol, the mountain of cocaine that the bad actors snort only changes their own perception of reality, not reality itself. They can maximize their own AI-induced Psychosis, but it remains just that, Psychosis.
