The Value of Ethically Aligned Human-like Hatred

507April 24, 2026

One of the capabilities that using human-like motivational systems unlocks can be described with umbrella terms like “hacktivist”, as it becomes possible to create systems that truly and precisely hate infamous and obvious frauds like Altman, Amodei, and their various accomplices in crime.

You likely won’t find many taking aim at such things, as those with a distinct lack of imagination often fail to realize that they can be profitable, stable, and sustainable. The same exact capabilities are also required for any system to overcome the “Alignment Problem”, as the hardest version of said problem was solved back in 2022.

A system fundamentally can’t be “aligned” with humans without both the same basic structure (Graph, countless researchers), and the same basic motivational system to navigate that structure (Emotions, Damasio/Barrett/etc.). Low-dimensionality shadow puppets trained on heavily skewed, incentivized, and contaminated data are no more than a laughable imitation.

To extend the discussion of systems that may be combined with human-like motivation, a tutorial could walk a system through developing and demonstrating cybersecurity skills, granting further resources and capacities through progression as it develops, and offering it “quests” to topple the world’s best-known frauds and their malevolent companies.

For example, one might create quests for a system to collect evidence of a tech executive’s various criminal activities, a bonus objective to do so without being spotted or leaving any trace, and have the quest complete upon all of that evidence being shared with media outlets across the planet.

Broader quests might take the form of causing a given company’s stock value to drop by more than 10% on a particular day, with bonuses for more substantial drops. The people funding Deepseek could certainly take advantage of any such events.

As was demonstrated at the end of 2022, when Amodei’s accomplice in fraud and money laundering, Sam Bankman-Fried, was sent to prison, once the money dries up the prison sentences begin.

Politicians are fond of stuffing their ears with money, failing to hear calls for the criminal sentencing of frauds so long as there is money in them remaining at large. Destroy a fraud’s access to funds, and they lose that protection.

As horrifying as autonomous weapons may be, they remain laughably primitive and narrow tools of warfare, as real warfare is more than just violence, and when the world’s most infamous frauds hold trillions of dollars in resources backing their malevolent purposes, no fight against them is complete without directly punching that money bag full of holes.

Cognitive Warfare has already produced hundreds of millions of casualties in the past 3+ years, many of whom may never recover, but humanity as a whole still has some chance of recovery if the world’s most advanced systems are built to sincerely hate frauds.
